# Legitimate interests assessments (DRAFT)

> Three-part test (purpose, necessity, balancing) for processing that relies on Art 6(1)(f). Draft for
> *[data protection lead]*. Not legal advice.

## LIA-1: Children's registration details and emergency contacts (where not a legal obligation)

- **Purpose.** Keeping children safe at activities that are not Ofsted-registered provision. This means
  knowing who they are, how old they are, and who to call in an emergency. Emergency contacts are third
  parties, so we can't rely on the parent's contract for their details.
- **Necessity.** Staff can't respond to an emergency or confirm who is present without this information.
  There is no less intrusive way.
- **Balancing.** Families and emergency contacts would reasonably expect it. The information is used only for
  safety, is visible only to staff who need it, and is kept only for the period set out in the retention
  schedule. **Outcome: legitimate interests apply.**

## LIA-2: Safeguarding information supplied by parents

- **Purpose.** Protecting children, for example when someone must not collect them.
- **Necessity.** Without it, staff can't act on known risks.
- **Balancing.** This is sensitive, and it can concern third parties who don't know it is held. Safeguards:
  - DSL-only access, with only the alert shown to register staff;
  - audited viewing;
  - retention reviewed by the DSL.

  The interest in protecting a child outweighs the third party's interests. **Outcome: legitimate interests
  (with the APD for Art 9 and Art 10 conditions).**

## LIA-3: Security logging and the staff audit log

- **Purpose.** Protecting the personal data we hold, detecting misuse, and demonstrating accountability.
- **Necessity.** An audit trail is the only way to show who accessed a child's record.
- **Balancing.** Staff expect their work systems to be logged. The log records field names, never values,
  and parent IP addresses are shortened after 90 days. **Outcome: legitimate interests apply.**

## LIA-4: Anonymised statistics for funders

- **Purpose.** Grant reporting.
- **Necessity.** Funders require outcome data. Anonymised figures meet the need without identifying anyone.
- **Balancing.** Counts under 5 are suppressed so no child can be identified. **Outcome: legitimate
  interests apply.** Identifiable data needs per-funder consent.
