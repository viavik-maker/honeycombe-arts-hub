Booking terms

These terms apply to every booking made with Honeycombe Arts Hub (Registered Charity No. 1127371), online or with our team. There is no membership fee.

Your booking

  • A place is yours once your booking shows as Confirmed in My bookings and in your email.
  • Some places need us to check them first (for example HAF funded places, or activities we confirm by hand). We hold the place while we check, and email you either way.
  • If a session is full you can join the waiting list. If a place comes up we'll email and text you and hold it for you for {{offer_hours}} hours (less if the session is very soon).
  • While you pay by card, your places are held for {{hold_minutes}} minutes.

Paying

  • You can pay by card online. Where we've agreed it, you can pay later by card, bank transfer, childcare vouchers or Tax-Free Childcare — quote your invoice number as the reference.
  • Invoices are due within {{terms_days}} days, and always before the first session they cover.
  • HAF funded places are free. If it turns out a child isn't eligible, we'll offer you a paid place instead.

Cancelling

  • You can cancel in My bookings up to {{cutoff_hours}} hours before a session starts.
  • Cancel at least {{refund_days}} days before and paid online by card: we refund your card. Otherwise, what you paid becomes credit on your account, used automatically on your next booking.
  • Less than {{cutoff_hours}} hours before, we can't refund — but please tell us in My bookings if your child can't come, so we know not to expect them.
  • If we have to cancel a session, you'll get your money back in full (to your card if you paid online, otherwise as account credit, or as a refund if you ask).

On the day

  • For drop-off sessions, children are only released to people you've told us can collect them, who give your collection password.
  • Please keep your child's health, allergy and contact details up to date in My family.
